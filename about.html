Ralli Cris Queda

Local QC resident and student sharing go-to mall tambayans across Quezon City based on personal experience.

Managing conditions like allergic rhinitis and eczema makes finding the right place to hang out a deliberate choice. High air pollution and hot environments can trigger rhinitis or cause excessive sweating that worsens eczema flare-ups.

Because of this, cool, well-ventilated, and air-conditioned indoor spaces become the ultimate safe haven. This guide was built to showcase accessible, fully air-conditioned spots across Quezon City where anyone can relax comfortably without overheating or breaking the bank.
