How I Choose These Places

Every spot listed here is budget-friendly, accessible via commute, and offers a relaxed vibe to unwind or hang out after class.

Quick Visitor Tips

  • Visit during off-peak hours (2 PM – 4 PM) for fewer crowds.
  • Check outdoor rooftop areas for fresh air and quieter seats.
